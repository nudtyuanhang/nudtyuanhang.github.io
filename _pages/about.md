Yuanhang Gao is a PhD candidate at the National University of Defense Technology (NUDT), advised by Prof. Xiangrui Yang and Prof. Dongsheng Li. He received his master's degree from NUDT in June 2025.

His research focuses on systems for AI, especially efficient and scalable LLM inference. To learn more about his work, see the [publications](/publications/) page.
